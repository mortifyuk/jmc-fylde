Landlord Fees and Charges

JMC Fylde Property Management Ltd provides residential letting and property management services to landlords across the Fylde Coast and surrounding areas. Our fee schedule is intended to help landlords understand the services provided and applicable charges before entering into an agreement.

View or download the JMC landlord fee schedule (PDF). Please contact us at jmcfyldepm@outlook.com if you need clarification about a fee or service.

Client Money Protection: JMC Fylde Property Management Ltd is a member of Safeagent. The Client Money Protection certificate is available to view or download (valid through 28 February 2027). The Safeagent accreditation certificate is also available to view or download (valid through 31 July 2027).

Redress scheme: JMC Fylde Property Management Ltd is a member of The Property Redress Scheme. The Property Redress Scheme certificate is available to view or download (membership valid through 15 March 2027).
