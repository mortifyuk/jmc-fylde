About us

A family-run business with a personal touch

JMC Fylde Property Management Ltd is a family-run property management company based in Blackpool, providing professional and personal property management services across Blackpool, Lytham St Annes and the wider Fylde Coast.

We believe property management should be straightforward, responsive and personal. Whether you need complete property management, tenant find and let-only services, short-term letting support or help with compliance and licensing, our aim is to give landlords confidence that their property is being looked after.

Professional management, local knowledge and a personal approach are at the heart of JMC.
