Complaints Procedure

We aim to provide a professional, reliable and friendly service. If something has gone wrong, please tell us so we can investigate.

How to make a complaint

Contact us at jmcfyldepm@outlook.com or write to JMC Fylde Property Management Ltd, 206 Dickson Road, Blackpool, FY1 2JS. Please explain what happened and the outcome you are seeking.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your complaint, investigate the issues raised and respond in accordance with our complaints procedure.

If you remain dissatisfied

After completing our internal complaints process, you may be able to refer your complaint to our redress scheme:

The Property Redress Scheme

We will provide information about the relevant escalation process and any applicable time limits when acknowledging or responding to your complaint.
